Our Animal Welfare Programs & Services

Safe Haven Animal Rescue operates four comprehensive community programs designed to rescue, care for, and protect vulnerable pets.

1. Animal Rescue & Veterinary Rehabilitation

Our team responds to reports of abandoned, injured, or neglected animals. Upon arrival at our shelter, every animal receives immediate medical intervention.

  • Emergency triage and veterinary treatment
  • Core vaccinations, deworming, and parasite treatment
  • Mandatory spay/neuter surgical procedures
  • Behavioral rehabilitation for trauma recovery

2. Pet Adoption & Matching Program

We operate a structured adoption workflow to ensure animals are placed in safe, compatible long-term homes.

  • Individual pet profile assessments and temperament testing
  • Prospective owner screening and property home-checks
  • Post-adoption follow-ups and transition support
Apply for Adoption

3. Temporary Foster Care Network

Our foster care network provides temporary, loving home environments for animals recovering from medical procedures or awaiting permanent adoption.

  • All food, bedding, and medical supplies provided to foster homes
  • Comprehensive guidance and emergency veterinary support
Become a Foster Parent

4. Community Education & Humane Outreach

We work directly with local schools, community centers, and neighborhood forums to promote responsible pet ownership and humane care.

  • Educational workshops on pet healthcare and nutrition
  • Subsidized vaccination and spay/neuter community drives
  • Youth engagement programs on animal empathy
