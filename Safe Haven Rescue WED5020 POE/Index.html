What is the dog and cat adoption fee? +
R850 includes vaccination, deworming, sterilization and microchip. Subsidized for seniors and includes lifetime support. This is a once-off cost.
Where are your animal shelters located? +
Cape Town Central Shelter: 12 Hope Street Gardens 8001. KZN Branch Shelter: 45 Main Road Gillitts 3610. Both open Mon-Sat. See interactive map below.
Can I foster if I work full-time or am a student? +
Yes! Many foster pets are cats needing only evening care. We provide FREE food, bedding and vet support. Foster period 2-8 weeks. Ideal for students.
