Our History & Origin - Since 2021 - Animal Rescue South Africa

Founded in response to growing crisis of animal homelessness in Cape Town.

Safe Haven Animal Rescue was founded in 2021 to tackle animal homelessness in Cape Town and KZN. What started as a small network of dedicated foster families has now developed into an established Non-Profit Organisation (NPO) providing structured and sustainable animal rescue services. We have rescued over 1,200 animals, with 850+ successful adoptions, and operate two shelters: Gardens Cape Town and Gillitts KZN.

Milestones: 2021 Founded as foster network → 2022 Registered NPO → 2023 Opened Cape Town Central Shelter 12 Hope Street → 2024 Opened KZN Branch → 2026 Part 3 Final website with JS and SEO.

Mission, Vision & Core Values - Animal Welfare NPO Principles

🎯

Our Mission - Rescue & Rehome

Rescue neglected and abandoned animals, provide comprehensive veterinary care, secure loving, responsible, permanent forever homes across South Africa.

👁️

Our Vision - No Pet Homeless

A society where no pet suffers neglect or homelessness, and animal welfare is recognized as integral to community well-being and responsible pet ownership in South Africa.

💚

Core Values - Compassion & Integrity

Compassion for all animals, integrity in all operations, community collaboration, dedication to humane care, responsible pet ownership education, transparency as NPO.
