Our History & Origin

Founded in 2021 to tackle animal homelessness in local communities.

Safe Haven Animal Rescue began as a small network of passionate foster families in response to rising numbers of abandoned stray pets. What started as temporary shelter care quickly grew into an established non-profit welfare organisation.


Today, our shelter operations provide emergency rescue, veterinary rehabilitation, community education, and structured pet adoptions across South Africa.

Mission, Vision & Core Values

The guiding principles behind every rescue operation we undertake.

🎯

Our Mission

To rescue neglected, abandoned, and surrendered animals, provide comprehensive veterinary care, and secure loving forever homes through responsible adoptions.

👁️

Our Vision

A compassionate society where no companion animal suffers from neglect, abuse, or homelessness, and every pet resides in a safe home.

💚

Core Values

Compassion for all life, integrity in shelter administration, community collaboration, and absolute dedication to animal welfare.
